FontBook is an offline type specimen and font inspector for Android.

Browse the font families supplied by your device, search by name or supported script, and preview each typeface with multilingual samples or your own text.

Explore each font in detail:

- Sample text and a full size waterfall
- Glyph repertoire and individual glyph metrics
- Alphabet, numeral, and punctuation specimens
- OpenType names, classifications, supported scripts, and file details
- Family grouping and script filters

FontBook has no ads, accounts, analytics, or network access. Font files and preview text stay on your device.
