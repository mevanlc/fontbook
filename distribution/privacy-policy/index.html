FontBook Privacy Policy

Last updated August 28, 2026

FontBook does not collect, transmit, share, or sell personal or sensitive user data. It has no ads, analytics, accounts, or network access.

FontBook reads font files supplied by Android from system font directories solely to display type samples, glyphs, metrics, and font metadata on your device. Font files and their metadata are not transmitted.

Text entered for custom previews and app settings remain on the device and are not transmitted. Uninstalling FontBook removes its app data.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if FontBook's data practices change. Material changes will be reflected here and in the policy shown inside the app.
